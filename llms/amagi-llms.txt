# Amagi

> Amagi delivers cloud-based infrastructure for the broadcast and streaming television industry — channel creation and playout, content distribution across linear/FAST/CTV, dynamic ad insertion and monetization, and viewership/ads analytics. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the provider's published OpenAPI and public developer surface.

## APIs

- [Mapsor API](https://mapsor.amagi.tv/docs/): Job orchestration and user/token management service (container job submit/status/logs, user provisioning). Auth: apiKey `token` query param. Production host: https://api.mapsor.amagi.tv
- [services-amagi-tv (Callisto) API](https://docs-callisto.amagi.tv/): Customer and key management service (create/delete customer, set/get/list keys and versions). Auth: apiKey headers `access_key` + `secret_key`. Production host: https://services.amagi.tv
